Common Questions

Things people ask us.

How long does a typical project take?

Most projects range from 6 to 14 weeks depending on scope. ERP systems and complex automation typically take longer than focused MVPs. We give you a realistic timeline upfront — not an optimistic one.

Do you work with clients outside Nepal?

Yes. We're based in Kawasoti, Nepal, but work remotely with clients globally. Communication happens over email, video calls, and shared project boards. Time zone is rarely a blocker.

What's your pricing model?

We scope projects individually. Most engagements are fixed-price per milestone, with an option for monthly retainer during active development. No hidden fees, no surprise invoices.

Do you provide ongoing support after launch?

Yes. Every project includes a support window after launch, and we offer extended maintenance plans. Production systems need care — we don't just drop code and leave.

Can you take over an existing codebase?

Often yes, after a technical audit. We'll assess code quality, architecture, and documentation before committing. If the codebase is a liability, we'll tell you honestly.

Do you build mobile apps?

Our primary focus is web platforms and backend systems. For mobile, we build responsive PWAs and can integrate with native shells when needed. We'll recommend the right approach for your use case.
